## Funnel copy (Search-to-AI)
### Top ~30 percent — SERP intent match
A custom booking system helps customers request or arrange service when the correct result depends on more than choosing a time. It can account for service type, location, availability, capacity, party or passenger details, lead time, approvals, deposits, and confirmation behavior.

KMX designs the booking path around the real business rules rather than forcing the business into a generic calendar.

### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
A digital business card tells customers to call during business hours. A generic scheduler may accept a time without collecting the details needed to serve it. Staff then recover missing information, correct invalid requests, or manually reconcile availability.

A stronger booking path shows the right choices, collects relevant details, and makes the status clear. Some businesses can confirm automatically. Others need a request-and-approval flow. Booking and customer intake remain separate owners because scheduling availability and gathering requirements are not the same job.
