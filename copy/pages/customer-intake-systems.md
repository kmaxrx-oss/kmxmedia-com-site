## Funnel copy (Search-to-AI)
### Top ~30 percent — SERP intent match
Quote and customer-intake systems help visitors explain what they need without beginning with an unstructured call or generic message. The interface can ask different questions by service, reveal requirements, collect documents or details when appropriate, and prepare a structured summary.

The goal is not to automate judgment that belongs to the business. It is to gather the repeatable facts before staff spend time reconstructing the request.

### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
A digital business card typically offers a telephone number and a contact form with one open message box. Customers do not know which details matter, and operators receive inquiries that cannot yet be evaluated. Long forms create the opposite problem by demanding every possible answer from every visitor.

Conditional intake asks only what the current answer makes relevant. It can provide a likely service path or pricing basis when rules support it, while reserving uncertain or discretionary work for human review. Booking remains a separate system because collecting requirements does not necessarily reserve time or capacity.

## FAQ
| Q | A |
|---|---|
| Can the system give an instant quote? | Only when pricing rules and inputs are stable enough. Otherwise it can show a pricing basis or collect what is needed for review. |
| Is intake the same as booking? | No. Intake gathers requirements. Booking assigns or requests time, capacity, or availability. |
| Can customers correct answers? | They should be able to review and correct important facts before submission. |
| Does artificial intelligence make the final decision? | Not by default. It can help ask, summarize, and route while human authority remains explicit. |
