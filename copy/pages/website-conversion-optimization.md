## Funnel copy (Search-to-AI)
### Top ~30 percent — SERP intent match
Website conversion optimization asks why existing visitors do not become useful calls, bookings, quote requests, intake submissions, or qualified conversations. KMX examines the match between visitor intent, page promise, choices, required information, and the next action.

The work is not simply making buttons brighter. It is removing uncertainty and unnecessary effort from the customer decision.

### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
A digital business card can receive traffic while leaving every important decision to the visitor. Services overlap, prices or requirements remain unexplained, mobile actions are buried, and a generic form collects too little information. The business receives fewer conversions or spends more time sorting weak ones.

Conversion work can clarify the primary action, separate visitor types, improve forms, introduce guided choices, or connect relevant proof to the decision. Search optimization brings the right audience; conversion optimization makes the page useful after arrival. A redesign is only necessary when the existing structure cannot support the required change.
