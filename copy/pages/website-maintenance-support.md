## Funnel copy (Search-to-AI)
### Top ~30 percent — SERP intent match
Website maintenance and support answer a continuing question: who checks updates, backups, forms, certificates, content changes, and small failures after launch? KMX scopes maintenance around the actual platform, business risk, and support responsibilities.

Maintenance is different from hosting. Hosting is where the site runs. Maintenance is the website-level work that keeps it dependable and current.

### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
Many websites become neglected because responsibility is implicit. The host assumes the owner handles the application. The owner assumes the original developer is watching it. Plugins, forms, certificates, and content change until a customer discovers the failure.

A maintenance arrangement should state what is monitored, what is updated, how backups are handled, what testing follows a change, which requests are included, and what falls outside routine care. A broken or compromised site may need repair before it can enter maintenance responsibly.

### Bottom ~30 percent — handoff into project brief /start/

Identify the platform, hosting provider, update responsibility, backup status, important forms or transactions, frequency of changes, known problems, and the kind of support the business expects.

The brief separates hosting responsibility, maintenance work, and new feature development so continuing care does not become an undefined promise.
