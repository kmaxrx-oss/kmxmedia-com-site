## Funnel copy (Search-to-AI)
### Top ~30 percent — SERP intent match
Custom website design is appropriate when a standard theme or generic form cannot express the required customer path. The site may need conditional services, location rules, capacity, scheduling, quote inputs, approval steps, deposits, customer intake, or information that changes what appears next.

KMX begins with the customer decision and the business rule behind it. Visual identity matters, but the defining question is what the interface must help someone understand or complete.

### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
Template sites become expensive when staff repeatedly compensate for what the interface cannot do. A generic form treats different services alike. A scheduling button may expose times without accounting for capacity. A page may explain several options without helping visitors choose.

Custom design turns those differences into a deliberate path. Questions appear only when relevant. Choices reveal requirements before submission. The site prepares structured information for the operator instead of forwarding an unqualified message. Not every custom-looking site needs custom development, so KMX separates visual customization from functional requirements.

### Bottom ~30 percent — handoff into project brief /start/

Describe what customers choose, what changes the next step, which information staff need, and where current tools force manual work. The brief organizes those answers into customer actions, business rules, possible integrations, and unresolved decisions.
