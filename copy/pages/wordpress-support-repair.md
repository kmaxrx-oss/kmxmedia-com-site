## Funnel copy (Search-to-AI)
### Top ~30 percent — SERP intent match
WordPress Support and Repair covers general WordPress problems: plugin conflicts, theme failures, broken forms, failed updates, layout damage, administrator access issues, configuration errors, and sites that nobody is confidently maintaining.

Begin with the public symptom and recent change. Do not guess the cause or send credentials through the public intake.

### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
WordPress combines core software, a theme, plugins, hosting, configuration, and content. A visible failure may originate in any one of those layers or in the interaction between them. Changing several layers at once can erase the evidence needed for a controlled repair.

The repair path should establish access, backups, recent changes, error behavior, and the smallest reversible test. If the screen shows the exact critical-error message, use the dedicated Critical Error page. If the site functions but needs continuing care, use Website Maintenance and Support.
