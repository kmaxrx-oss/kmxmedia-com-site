### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
A website can add keywords without becoming more useful. Pages compete with one another, headings repeat broad terms, and visitors land on thin content that sends everyone to the same contact form. That is not a durable search or conversion strategy.

KMX separates broad organic visibility from local search and post-click conversion. Search Engine Optimization owns what pages should be discovered and why. Local Search Engine Optimization owns nearby discovery and business-location relevance. Website Conversion Optimization owns what happens after the click. These functions support one another without becoming one indistinct promise.
