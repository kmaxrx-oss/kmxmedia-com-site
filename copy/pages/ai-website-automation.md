## Funnel copy (Search-to-AI)
### Top ~30 percent — SERP intent match
Artificial intelligence website automation can help with first response, missing-information collection, guided questions, summarization, routing, reminders, and preparation for human follow-up. KMX applies it to specific workflow jobs rather than using a generic chatbot as the entire strategy.

The useful question is not whether a website has artificial intelligence. It is which repeatable interaction can become faster or clearer without obscuring responsibility.

### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
A digital business card leaves every question for staff. A generic chatbot can create a different failure: plausible conversation without a dependable record, explicit authority, or useful handoff. The visitor may talk more while the operator learns less.

A stronger system gives automation bounded jobs. It can identify missing information, explain known choices, assemble a brief, and recognize when a person needs to take over. Important facts should survive outside the transcript in a visible, correctable record. Pricing, approval, availability, and promises must remain tied to authoritative rules.

The brief separates conversational assistance from durable business authority.

## FAQ

| Do I need a chatbot? | No. Artificial intelligence may support a form, planner, follow-up step, or operator tool without becoming a free-form chatbot. |
| Can it answer every customer question? | It should answer only within approved information and hand off when the question exceeds that boundary. |
| Can it qualify leads? | It can apply explicit criteria and summarize information. Final authority must be defined by the business. |
| Will it replace staff? | The selected use is to reduce repetitive work and prepare better handoffs, not to promise replacement of human judgment. |
