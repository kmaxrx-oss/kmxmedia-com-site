### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
Visual design matters, but a polished image cannot prove that a booking path handles real requirements or that an intake form gives the operator usable information. KMX case-study structure therefore begins with the workflow: what the customer needed to do, what the business previously handled manually, what the interface changed, and what remained a human decision.

Where results, quotes, or private operating details are not approved, the page should say less rather than invent proof. Process transparency and the live KMX project brief also demonstrate how requirements become a plan.
