### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
A Google Business Profile can generate impressions, calls, and website visits, but it cannot compensate for unclear services or a weak destination. The website can also undermine local visibility when business facts conflict, important services lack clear pages, or every location is represented by duplicated text.

KMX treats the profile and website as connected surfaces with different jobs. The profile supports discovery and immediate actions. The site explains services, earns deeper relevance, and handles more complex decisions. Claims about addresses, service areas, hours, and locations must remain truthful and consistent.
