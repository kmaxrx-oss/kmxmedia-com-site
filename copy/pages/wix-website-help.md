## Funnel copy (Search-to-AI)
### Top ~30 percent — SERP intent match
Wix Website Help is for sites with broken layouts, mobile display problems, forms that do not work, domain confusion, unfinished do-it-yourself builds, application setup problems, or earlier edits that need to be understood or reversed.

Because Wix controls the hosting platform, the repair question is usually about site configuration, content, applications, domains, or custom behavior rather than direct server repair.

### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
A functioning Wix site can still fail as a customer path. Pages may look acceptable on desktop while becoming difficult on mobile. Forms may collect weak information. Wix Bookings or Stores may not match the business process. Search settings and page structure may leave important services unclear.

The correct disposition is not always migration. The site may be repairable within Wix, may need a redesign in Wix, or may have requirements better served elsewhere. That decision should follow the business rules, working content, platform constraints, and cost of replacement.
