## Funnel copy (Search-to-AI)
### Top ~30 percent — SERP intent match
A website redesign should solve more than an aging visual style. It should clarify services, improve the mobile experience, strengthen the path from search to action, and remove friction preventing visitors from calling, booking, requesting a quote, or completing intake.

KMX evaluates redesign work from the customer path backward. We identify what deserves to survive, what creates confusion, and what the business now needs the site to accomplish.

### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
Many redesigns replace colors, typography, and layouts while preserving the same digital-business-card behavior. The updated site looks newer but still asks everyone to call, collects weak inquiries, and leaves important choices unexplained.

The redesign decision should separate presentation, search-discovery, conversion, and functional problems. That determines whether the work is content restructuring, conversion optimization, new workflow behavior, or repair before redesign. Existing URLs, useful content, search value, business rules, and working integrations should not be discarded simply because the visual direction changes.
