## Funnel copy (Search-to-AI)
### Top ~30 percent — SERP intent match
Small-business website design should make a limited team more effective. The site needs to explain the offer, help customers find the business, and make the next action clear without creating more administrative work behind the scenes.

KMX Media builds around practical outcomes: qualified calls, better quote requests, scheduling, customer intake, local search conversion, and follow-up. The right scope may be a focused website with a few strong pages or a custom workflow with business rules.

### Middle ~40 percent — why ordinary sites fail this job
Small businesses often inherit websites designed around sections instead of work. The home page introduces the company, the service page lists capabilities, and the contact page asks for a name and email. The owner still has to determine what the person needs, whether the request fits, what details are missing, and how quickly it requires attention.

A stronger website takes responsibility for the repeatable part of that process. It can ask service-specific questions, separate urgent requests from planned projects, capture details that affect a quote, or help customers request an appointment. Small-business design does not mean a smaller corporate website. It means deciding carefully where every page, question, and integration earns its place.
