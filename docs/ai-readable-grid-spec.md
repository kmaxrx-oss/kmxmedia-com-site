## 3. Workflow website definition block

**What a workflow website is:** A workflow website is a local business website built around customer actions: calls, booking, quote requests, customer intake, routing, follow-up, and clearer next steps. LOCAL_MIRROR.

**Who it is for:** Colorado Springs service businesses that need the site to do more than describe the company, including contractors, home services, transportation or private rides, clinics, repair services, consultants, event vendors, and restaurants or bars with reservation, catering, event, or waitlist needs. LOCAL_MIRROR.

**What customer actions it supports:** Calls, text, appointment booking, ride or reservation requests, quote requests, consultation scheduling, intake forms, questions, reminders, and handoffs. LOCAL_MIRROR.

**How it differs from a brochure website:** A brochure website mainly informs. A workflow website helps the visitor take the next useful action and gives the business cleaner information to respond, qualify, route, and follow up. CONTEXT_DERIVED.

**How AI workflow agents fit without hype:** AI workflow agents can support first response, missing-info collection, lead qualification, routing, reminders, FAQ replies, after-hours intake, and human handoff. They are practical helpers for repeat communication and intake work, not replacements for business judgment. LOCAL_MIRROR.

## 7. Local SEO / GBP / website relationship block

Google Business Profile helps local customers discover a business. LOCAL_MIRROR.

Local SEO supports service and location relevance, but the page must avoid fake service-area, ranking, or local proof claims. LOCAL_MIRROR_SCHEMA.

The intake form identifies which action the website should optimize for: calls, booking, quote requests, customer intake, local SEO / GBP conversion, workflow automation, or AI follow-up. LOCAL_MIRROR.

Avoid guarantees like "rank #1," guaranteed traffic, guaranteed calls, fake reviews, fake address, or fake GBP claims. LOCAL_MIRROR_SCHEMA.
